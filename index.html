Topics

Getting started

What is Echoes of Angmar?

A fan-run server for The Lord of the Rings Online that runs the game as it was during Shadows of Angmar, before the Mines of Moria expansion. It is not run by or connected to Standing Stone Games.

Does it cost anything?

No. Echoes of Angmar is free to play and has no store. If we ever ask for donations to cover hosting, they will never buy anything in game.

What do I need to play?

A Windows PC, a copy of the game client and the Echoes of Angmar launcher. You can download both after you create an account.

How do I install it?
  1. Create an account on this site and confirm your email.
  2. Download the launcher and client from your account page.
  3. Run the launcher and point it at the client folder.
  4. Sign in with your account name and password.

If the launcher will not start, the #tech-help channel in Discord can walk you through it.

Accounts & characters

I played before the hand-over. Do I need a new account?

No. Existing accounts and characters carry over. Sign in with the same account name and password you used before.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page. We will email a reset link to the address on your account.

Can I have more than one account?

Yes, up to two per person. Running more than one character at the same time in group content is covered by the rules in Discord.

How do I delete my account?

From your account page, or by asking a staff member in Discord. Deleting an account removes its characters and cannot be undone.

The game

Which version of the game is this?

The Shadows of Angmar era, before Mines of Moria. The level cap is 50 and the world ends where it did then.

Can I play a Warden or Runekeeper?

No. Both classes arrived with Mines of Moria. You can play the seven original classes: Burglar, Captain, Champion, Guardian, Hunter, Lore-master and Minstrel.

Is everything working?

Not quite. The server is a recreation, so some quests, skills and instances still have bugs. Report anything you find in #bug-reports on Discord, with your character name and where it happened.

Is there a cash shop?

No. Shadows of Angmar never had one, and neither do we.

Community

Where do players talk?

In Discord. That is where we post news, downtime, events and patch notes, and where staff answer questions.

Are there rules?

Yes, and they are short: treat people well, do not cheat or abuse bugs, and keep names fitting for Middle-earth. The full rules are pinned in Discord.

Can I help run the server?

Yes. We are looking for developers, game masters and people who can write, test and moderate. Say hello in #volunteers.

The hand-over

Why did the server change hands?

The original developers retired from the project in October 2026 and asked the community for someone to carry it on. We stepped up.

Will there be downtime?

Yes, while the server moves to new hardware. We will announce the date and expected length in Discord at least a week in advance.

Is my character safe?

Yes. Account and character data come across with the server. If anything looks wrong after the move, tell us in Discord and we will check it against the backup.

Still stuck?

Ask in Discord. Someone who has been through it will usually answer within the hour.
